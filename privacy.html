Privacy policy

How VB Coach handles your information.

About this policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026. This policy describes the VB Coach app and this support website, provided by Alex Keist. For privacy questions or requests, contact steadyappsupport@gmail.com.

Data on your device

VB Coach stores your coach and team profile, roster, practice plans and notes, favorites, completion dates, scoreboard state, and timer progress locally. The app does not require an account or provide automatic account-based synchronization. Device backups may include app data according to your operating-system settings.

VB Coach does not send your roster, practices, or coaching notes to a developer-operated server. The app does not include a developer-operated analytics or advertising service.

Files you export and share

You can export a backup of saved plans, profile, roster, favorites, and completion dates. VB Coach does not encrypt these files. You choose where to save or share them. iCloud Drive and other file providers handle exported files under their own terms and privacy policies. Practice and calendar sharing use the destination you choose.

YouTube and internet connections

In version 1.0 build 4 and later, browsing drills uses artwork bundled in the app and does not request YouTube thumbnails or load an embedded player. Tutorial links open YouTube or your browser only when you choose to open them. Those external services may receive your IP address and device or browser information and process data under their own policies. Watching videos requires internet access. Earlier TestFlight builds could request YouTube thumbnails while browsing.

See Google’s privacy policy and YouTube’s terms. The current standard app configuration uses bundled drill content and has no production remote-library endpoint configured.

This website and support email

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded videos.

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Email providers process messages as part of delivering and storing correspondence. Please avoid unnecessary player information. Contact steadyappsupport@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete practices and roster entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to system backups. Exported files and copies shared with others remain in their chosen destinations until you or the recipients delete them. Manage device backups through your system settings.

If you enter information about players, including children, include only what you need for coaching and control access to your device and exported backups.

Policy updates

This page will be updated when data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
