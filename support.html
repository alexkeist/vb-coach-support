Support

Help with your plans, backups, team, and practice timers.

Get in touch

For app support or privacy requests, email steadyappsupport@gmail.com. VB Coach is provided by Alex Keist.

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Avoid sending player names, private coaching notes, or backup files unless needed and specifically requested.

Back up or move your data

Open Team → Backup & Restore → Export Backup, then choose a location in Files. Backups contain saved plans, profile, roster, favorites, and completion dates. They include player names and coaching notes and are not encrypted by VB Coach; store them somewhere you trust.

On another device, choose Restore from Backup, review the file, and tap Restore Missing Data. Existing plans remain unchanged. Missing players are added and favorites are combined. Conflicting jersey numbers are left blank on added players.

Unsaved drafts, active timers, scoreboard scores, and videos are not transferred. There is no automatic account-based synchronization.

Reuse a practice

Open a saved practice and tap Duplicate Practice. Adjust its date, name, or drills and save. The original practice remains unchanged.

Recover an interrupted practice

Use Resume Practice on Today or open the saved practice. Recovered timers open paused. Timers also pause when you leave the runner or the app becomes inactive. Changing drill order or durations invalidates saved progress for that practice.

Offline use and videos

Saved plans, your roster, and bundled drill instructions work offline. YouTube thumbnails and tutorials need internet access. Video availability can change independently of VB Coach.

Storage problems

Keep the app installed if saved data cannot be opened. Deleting it can remove local data. Try closing and reopening the app, and keep a recent exported backup. Contact support if the problem continues.
